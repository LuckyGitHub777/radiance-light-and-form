# Radiance — Light and Form

**Explore online:** [Open Radiance](https://luckygithub777.github.io/radiance-light-and-form/), or enter the [Light Room](https://luckygithub777.github.io/radiance-light-and-form/light-room.html) for a luminous, leave-on visual composition.

Radiance is an interactive work of light and geometry. The main studio offers Halo, Bloom and Spiral forms, optional gentle movement, a short focus timer, and a transparent estimate calculator. The separate Light Room presents original Pyramid, Halo, Bloom and Prism compositions with six color palettes (including default multicolor Spectrum) and adjustable visual brightness.

**Offline Light Room:** Open the live Light Room and select **Save offline copy**. Transfer the saved single HTML file by USB and open it in a browser, even without internet. All visuals, controls and scripts are embedded in the file. The page makes no background network requests. **Continuous Light** flows by default when motion is permitted. **Spectrum** (default color) uses seven moving hues in the light field while preserving a coordinated two-color geometric outline; **Linear** (default pattern) uses moving segmented horizontal marks; choose **Organic** for the earlier curved waves. Choose **Drift / Flow / Vivid / Surge** to adjust pace, or **Pause light flow** to keep the composition still. Reduced-motion preferences turn animation off. Very old browsers and devices may render gradients differently.

**Light Room controls:** Pick a form, color and Linear/Organic pattern, choose Soft/Warm/Radiant brightness, adjust **Flow pace**, optionally pause the animation, then select **Hide controls** or **Fullscreen**. Keep the laptop cool, take screen breaks and let its display sleep when appropriate. No attempt is made to prevent operating-system screen sleep.

Bloom's seven equal-radius circles follow a mathematically precise **Seed of Life** construction: six centers form a regular hexagon around the seventh, with adjacent center spacing equal to circle radius. **Surge** moves patterns faster without adding intentional strobe effects. Screen displays are not calibrated light-therapy devices; no medical benefits are asserted or guaranteed.

It makes no claim to reproduce or deliver the health effects attributed to commercial light-system hardware. Any values in the main studio's calculator are estimates, not measured earnings.
