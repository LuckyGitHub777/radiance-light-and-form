# Radiance — Light and Form

**Explore online:** [Open Radiance](https://luckygithub777.github.io/radiance-light-and-form/), or enter the [Light Room](https://luckygithub777.github.io/radiance-light-and-form/light-room.html) for a luminous, leave-on visual composition.

Radiance is an interactive work of light and geometry. The main studio offers Halo, Bloom and Spiral forms, optional gentle movement, a short focus timer, and a transparent estimate calculator. The separate Light Room presents original Pyramid, Halo, Bloom and Prism compositions with five color palettes and adjustable visual brightness.

**Offline Light Room:** Open the live Light Room and select **Save offline copy**. Transfer the saved single HTML file by USB and open it in a browser, even without internet. All visuals, controls and scripts are embedded in the file. The page makes no background network requests, and motion is off by default. Very old browsers and devices may render gradients differently.

**Light Room controls:** Pick a form and color, choose Soft/Warm/Radiant brightness, optionally enable slow motion, then select **Hide controls** or **Fullscreen**. Keep the laptop cool, take screen breaks and let its display sleep when appropriate. No attempt is made to prevent operating-system screen sleep.

Radiance is original visual art and ambient decoration, **not** a medical, therapeutic, financial, or scientific instrument. It makes no claim to reproduce or deliver the health effects attributed to commercial light-system hardware. Any values in the main studio's calculator are estimates, not measured earnings.
