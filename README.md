# Radiance — Light and Form

**Explore online:** [Open Radiance](https://luckygithub777.github.io/radiance-light-and-form/), or enter the [Light Room](https://luckygithub777.github.io/radiance-light-and-form/light-room.html) for a luminous, leave-on visual composition.

Radiance is an interactive work of light and geometry. The main studio offers Halo, Bloom and Spiral forms, optional gentle movement, a short focus timer, and a transparent estimate calculator. The separate Light Room presents original Pyramid, Sphere, Seed of Life, Flower of Life, Cube and Prism compositions with six color palettes (including default multicolor Spectrum) and adjustable visual brightness.

**Offline Light Room:** Open the live Light Room and select **Save offline copy**. Transfer the saved single HTML file by USB and open it in a browser, even without internet. All visuals, controls and scripts are embedded in the file. The page makes no background network requests. **Continuous Light** flows by default when motion is permitted. **Spectrum** (default color) uses seven moving hues in the light field while preserving a coordinated two-color geometric outline; **Segments** (default pattern) uses moving structured marks; choose **Waves** for the earlier curved flow. Choose **Flow / Vivid / Surge** to adjust pace, or **Pause light flow** to keep the composition still. Reduced-motion preferences turn animation off. Very old browsers and devices may render gradients differently.

**Light Room controls:** A centered, ready-to-use panel: select a form and palette, then Segments/Waves, Soft/Warm/Radiant glow, and Flow/Vivid/Surge pace. Defaults are Spectrum, Segments, Radiant, and Surge. Pause, Hide controls, and Fullscreen are centered below the choices. Hidden controls disappear completely until pointer movement, touch, or keyboard focus reveals the restore affordance; Escape also restores controls. Keep the laptop cool, take screen breaks and let its display sleep when appropriate. No attempt is made to prevent operating-system screen sleep.

The **Seed** form's seven equal-radius circles follow a mathematically precise **Seed of Life** construction: six centers form a regular hexagon around the seventh, with adjacent center spacing equal to circle radius. The **Flower** form uses 19 congruent circles on a hexagonal lattice, while **Cube** adds three geometric faces with independent light fields. **Surge** moves patterns faster without adding intentional strobe effects. Screen displays are not calibrated light-therapy devices; no medical benefits are asserted or guaranteed.
