About the experience

A quiet visual workspace.

Radiance presents original geometric visuals with optional slow movement, a session timer, a single-action focus panel, and a transparent cost calculator.

This is an ambient visual and productivity interface, not a medical treatment or a substitute for professional care. It does not measure health effects or provide therapeutic claims.

Lightweight by design

Static first. Offline by design.

No frameworks, videos, WebGL, external fonts, libraries, tracking, analytics, network requests, credentials or persistent browser storage. Motion is off by default, has one deliberately slow transform, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

This static page works offline or over HTTPS. It has no accounts or synchronization.
